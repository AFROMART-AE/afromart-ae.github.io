ABOUT AFROMART

A trading partner built around quality, access and trust.

AFROMART is a long-standing African foodstuffs, beverages and cosmetics trading business serving customers and wholesale buyers in the UAE. Our experience is built around genuine African products, dependable sourcing and a strong understanding of the needs of African-product customers in the region.

Food safety and hygiene are central to our operations. We maintain HACCP certification and place strong emphasis on hygienic handling, packing and product care for applicable food products, helping us deliver with consistency, cleanliness and confidence.

OUR QUALITY COMMITMENT

Experience backed by food safety & hygiene.

As an established African foodstuff, beverages and cosmetics business, we understand that quality is more than the product itself. It also means responsible handling, hygienic packing and careful attention to food-safety practices.
